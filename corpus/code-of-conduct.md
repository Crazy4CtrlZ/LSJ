# LSJ, Inc. — Code of Conduct and Workplace Behavior Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Code of Conduct sets expectations for professional behavior at LSJ. It applies to all employees, contractors, and interns in all work contexts: offices, remote work, business travel, company events, and company communication channels.

## 2. Core Standards

Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect, protect company and customer information (POL-007), avoid conflicts of interest, and comply with applicable laws in every jurisdiction where the Company operates.

## 3. Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination

### 3.1 Prohibited conduct

The Company prohibits harassment, discrimination, and retaliation based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law. Prohibited conduct includes unwelcome advances, degrading jokes or slurs, exclusionary behavior, and hostile communication in any channel, including chat and video tools.

### 3.2 Zero tolerance for retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation is itself a serious violation, regardless of the outcome of the original report.

## 4. Reporting Concerns

### 4.1 Channels

Concerns may be reported through any of the following:

1. Direct manager (unless the concern involves the manager).
2. Any member of People Operations.
3. The confidential Ethics Line (web form or phone), which supports anonymous reports.

### 4.2 Handling of sensitive reports

Reports involving harassment, discrimination, violence, or safety are always routed to a human case manager in People Operations. Automated systems, including the HR assistant, must not attempt to resolve such reports; they should acknowledge the concern, provide these reporting channels, and escalate by opening an HR case marked "Sensitive — Human Review."

### 4.3 Investigation

People Operations acknowledges reports within 2 business days, investigates promptly and as confidentially as possible, and communicates outcomes to the reporter to the extent appropriate.

## 5. Workplace Safety and Violence Prevention

Threats, intimidation, and violence are prohibited. Imminent-danger situations should go to local emergency services first, then the Ethics Line. Weapons are prohibited on company premises and at company events.

## 6. Substance Use

Employees must not work under the impairment of alcohol or drugs. Moderate alcohol consumption at company-sanctioned events is permitted where legal; expense rules for alcohol are in POL-003, Section 5.

## 7. Integrity of Records and Fraud

Falsifying timesheets, expense reports (POL-003), business records, or credentials is grounds for discipline up to termination. Suspected fraud should be reported through the channels in Section 4.

## 8. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose to People Operations: outside employment with competitors or vendors, financial interests in companies that do business with LSJ, romantic relationships within a reporting line, and acceptance of gifts above $100 in value from business partners.

## 9. Communication and Social Media

Employees may not speak on behalf of the Company without authorization from Communications. Confidential information (POL-007, Section 3) must never be shared publicly. Professional-respect standards in Section 3 apply to all company communication channels.

## 10. Disciplinary Process

Violations are addressed proportionately: coaching, written warning, final warning, or termination. Serious violations (harassment, violence, fraud, major security breaches) may result in immediate termination. The disciplinary process is administered by People Operations with department leadership.
