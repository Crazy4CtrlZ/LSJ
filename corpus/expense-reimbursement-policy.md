# LSJ, Inc. — Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs business expenses incurred by LSJ employees and the process for reimbursement. It applies to all regular employees. Contractors invoice expenses per their service agreements and are not covered here. Business travel booking rules (flights, lodging, per diems) are detailed in the Travel Policy (POL-008); this policy governs reimbursement mechanics and non-travel expenses.

## 2. General Principles

1. Expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and incurred for legitimate business purposes.
2. Employees must use the most economical option that meets the business need.
3. Itemized receipts are required for all expenses of $25 or more.
4. Personal expenses are never reimbursable, even if incurred during business travel.
5. Falsified expense claims are treated as fraud under the Code of Conduct (POL-005, Section 7).

## 3. Submission and Approval

### 3.1 Submission window

Expense reports must be submitted in LSJ Expense within 30 calendar days of the date the expense was incurred. Reports submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed except with VP-level approval.

### 3.2 Approval chain

| Expense report total | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $500 | Direct manager |
| $500.01 – $5,000 | Manager + department head |
| Over $5,000 | Manager + department head + Finance Controller |

### 3.3 Pre-approval

Any single item over $500, all conference registrations, and all client entertainment require written pre-approval in LSJ Expense before the expense is incurred.

### 3.4 Reimbursement timing

Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next semi-monthly payroll run, typically within 15 calendar days of final approval.

## 4. Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times per year may request a corporate card. Corporate card charges must still be submitted and coded in LSJ Expense within 30 days. Personal use of corporate cards is prohibited; accidental personal charges must be flagged and repaid within 10 business days.

## 5. Meals and Entertainment

- Business meals with external parties: reimbursable with attendee list and business purpose; cap of $100 per person for dinner, $50 for lunch.
- Team meals: require manager pre-approval; cap of $40 per person.
- Alcohol: not reimbursable, except during client entertainment pre-approved by a VP.
- Travel meal per diems are set in the Travel Policy (POL-008, Section 5) and replace itemized meal reimbursement while traveling.

## 6. Technology and Equipment Purchases

Employees may not purchase laptops, monitors, phones, or peripherals for reimbursement. All computing equipment is provisioned by IT under the Onboarding and Equipment Policy (POL-006, Section 5). Exceptions: cables and adapters up to $50 per year may be expensed without pre-approval.

Software purchases and SaaS subscriptions of any amount require IT approval before purchase to ensure security review (POL-007, Section 8) and license compliance.

## 7. Home Office Expenses

### 7.1 Stipend

Eligible hybrid and fully-remote employees receive:

- A one-time home office setup stipend of $500, available in the first 90 days of employment.
- An annual refresh allowance of $250 per calendar year, non-cumulative.

### 7.2 Eligible items

The stipend covers desks, chairs (up to $300 per chair), lighting, keyboards/mice if not IT-provisioned, and ergonomic accessories. Monitors are excluded — they are IT-provisioned under POL-006, Section 5.1. Purchases are made personally and claimed through LSJ Expense with receipts, coded "Home Office Stipend."

### 7.3 Internet

Hybrid and fully-remote employees may claim up to $40 per month toward home internet, submitted quarterly with a bill copy.

### 7.4 Ineligible

Rent, utilities (other than the internet allowance), coffee, coworking memberships without VP approval, and furniture beyond the stipend caps are not reimbursable.

## 8. Professional Development

With manager pre-approval, employees may expense up to $1,500 per year for courses, books, certifications, and conference fees (conference travel follows POL-008). Tuition reimbursement for degree programs is a separate benefit (POL-004, Section 8).

## 9. Non-Reimbursable Items (Summary)

Personal entertainment, commuting costs between home and the assigned office, traffic fines, airline seat upgrades beyond policy class (POL-008, Section 3), personal-device accessories, gifts between employees, and any expense lacking a required receipt or pre-approval.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I buy a laptop and expense it?**
A: No. All computing equipment is IT-provisioned (Section 6; POL-006, Section 5). Only cables and adapters up to $50/year may be expensed.

**Q: Can I expense a home office chair?**
A: Yes, up to $300 per chair through your home office stipend (Section 7.2), if you are an eligible hybrid or fully-remote employee with stipend budget remaining (Section 7.1).

**Q: I lost a receipt for a $30 expense. Can I still claim it?**
A: Itemized receipts are required at $25 and above (Section 2). Without one, the expense is not reimbursable (Section 9); ask the merchant for a duplicate.

**Q: How long do I have to submit an expense?**
A: 30 calendar days from the expense date; after 60 days reimbursement requires VP approval (Section 3.1).

**Q: Is alcohol at a team dinner reimbursable?**
A: No — alcohol is only reimbursable for client entertainment with VP pre-approval (Section 5).
