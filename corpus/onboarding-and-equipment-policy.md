# LSJ, Inc. — Onboarding and Equipment Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes the onboarding program for new LSJ employees and the provisioning, use, and return of company equipment. It applies to all employees; contractors receive equipment only when specified in their service agreement.

## 2. Before Day One

Once an offer is signed, People Operations initiates pre-boarding:

1. Background check and right-to-work verification must clear before the start date.
2. IT ships equipment (Section 5) to arrive by the start date for remote hires, or stages it at the assigned office desk for office-based hires.
3. The new hire receives portal credentials for payroll, benefits enrollment (POL-004, Section 4.1 — 30-day deadline), and required policy acknowledgments.

## 3. Onboarding Program

### 3.1 First week

Day one includes IT setup verification, security training enrollment (Section 4), and a manager kickoff meeting. During the first week, the new hire meets their onboarding buddy — a peer assigned by the manager for the first 90 days.

### 3.2 30/60/90 plan

Managers must publish a written 30/60/90-day plan within the first week. Checkpoints: 30-day informal check-in, 60-day progress review, 90-day introductory-period review (POL-010, Section 3). The 90-day date also ends the PTO usage restriction (POL-001, Section 2.3) and starts 401(k) matching (POL-004, Section 5).

### 3.3 Required training

Within 30 days, all new hires must complete: Security Awareness (POL-007), Code of Conduct acknowledgment (POL-005), and anti-harassment training. Managers additionally complete manager-essentials training within 60 days.

## 4. Accounts and Access

Access follows least-privilege defaults by role. Additional system access requires manager approval and, for systems containing Restricted data, Security approval (POL-007, Section 4). Multi-factor authentication enrollment is mandatory on day one.

## 5. Equipment Provisioning

### 5.1 Standard kit

Every employee receives a company laptop (choice of standard configurations), and office-based employees receive a monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset at their desk. Fully-remote and hybrid employees may request one IT-provisioned external monitor for home use.

### 5.2 Laptop refresh

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or earlier for verified performance or security issues. Employees may not purchase computing equipment for reimbursement (POL-003, Section 6).

### 5.3 Mobile devices

Roles with an on-call or field requirement may be issued a company phone with department head approval. Otherwise, employees may enroll a personal phone in mobile device management (MDM) to access company email under POL-007, Section 7.

## 6. Equipment Use and Care

Company equipment is for business use, with incidental personal use permitted under POL-005 and POL-007. Employees must not disable security controls (encryption, MDM, endpoint protection). Loss or theft must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours (POL-007, Section 9). Devices must not be left unattended in vehicles or checked luggage during travel (POL-008, Section 7).

Home-office furniture and accessories are funded through the stipend in POL-003, Section 7 — they are employee-owned. IT-provisioned equipment remains company property.

## 7. Equipment Return

Upon separation or extended unpaid leave beyond 30 days, all company equipment must be returned within 10 business days using prepaid shipping materials provided by IT. Unreturned equipment value may be recovered as permitted by law.

## 8. Workspace Standards for Remote Work

Remote workspaces must have reliable internet (minimum 25 Mbps down / 5 Mbps up recommended), a lockable or otherwise private working area for calls involving Confidential information, and compliance with POL-007, Section 5 network rules. Internet cost sharing is covered by POL-003, Section 7.3.
