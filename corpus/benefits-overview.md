# LSJ, Inc. — Benefits Overview

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document summarizes the benefits programs available to LSJ employees, eligibility rules, and enrollment procedures. Plan documents and insurance certificates govern in case of any conflict with this summary. Benefits described here apply to U.S.-based employees; international offices publish local supplements through People Operations.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligible employees

Regular full-time employees (30 or more scheduled hours per week) are eligible for the full benefits program. Regular part-time employees (20–29 hours) are eligible for medical coverage only, at prorated employer contribution.

### 2.2 Ineligible groups

Independent contractors, agency workers, and interns are not eligible for Company benefits programs. Interns receive a wellness stipend described in their internship agreement.

### 2.3 Coverage start

Coverage begins on the first day of the month following the hire date. An employee hired on March 15 is covered from April 1; an employee hired on March 1 is covered from April 1 as well (coverage never begins mid-month).

### 2.4 Dependents

Eligible dependents include a spouse or domestic partner and children up to age 26. Documentation is required within 30 days of enrollment.

## 3. Medical, Dental, and Vision

The Company offers three medical plans (Base PPO, Premium PPO, HDHP with HSA), one dental plan, and one vision plan. Employer contribution covers 80% of employee-only premiums and 60% of dependent premiums on the Base PPO; other plans are benchmarked against that contribution. HDHP enrollees receive an employer HSA seed of $750 (employee-only) or $1,500 (family) per year.

## 4. Enrollment

### 4.1 New hires

New hires must complete enrollment in the benefits portal within 30 days of their hire date. Missing the window means waiting for open enrollment unless a qualifying life event occurs.

### 4.2 Open enrollment

Open enrollment runs November 1–15 each year, with elections effective January 1.

### 4.3 Qualifying life events

Marriage, divorce, birth or adoption, loss of other coverage, and relocation affecting plan availability allow election changes within 30 days of the event, with documentation.

## 5. Retirement — 401(k)

Employees may contribute to the 401(k) plan from their first paycheck. The Company matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of eligible salary, beginning after 90 days of employment. Matching contributions vest 100% immediately. Both traditional and Roth options are available.

## 6. Benefits During Leave

### 6.1 Paid leave

During any paid leave (PTO, sick leave, parental leave under POL-001), benefits continue unchanged and employee premium shares continue via payroll deduction.

### 6.2 Unpaid leave

During approved unpaid personal leave (POL-001, Section 7), medical coverage continues for up to 30 days with the employee paying the employee share directly. Beyond 30 days, continuation coverage rules (COBRA or local equivalent) apply.

## 7. Insurance and Income Protection

- Basic life insurance at 1x annual salary, Company-paid; supplemental coverage up to 4x available at employee cost.
- Short-term disability: 60% of salary for up to 12 weeks after a 7-day waiting period, Company-paid.
- Long-term disability: 60% of salary after 90 days, Company-paid.

## 8. Additional Benefits

- Tuition reimbursement: up to $3,000 per calendar year for degree programs relevant to the employee's role, available after 12 months of service, with manager and People Operations pre-approval. Grades of B or better required for reimbursement. (Professional development courses under $1,500/year are expensed instead — see POL-003, Section 8.)
- Wellness stipend: $300 per year for gym memberships, fitness equipment, or mental-wellness apps, claimed through LSJ Expense.
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): free confidential counseling, 8 sessions per issue per year, available to all employees and household members from day one.
- Commuter benefit: pre-tax transit/parking deductions up to IRS limits for office-assigned employees.

## 9. Benefits Questions and Escalation

Plan-selection guidance, claims disputes, and eligibility edge cases should be raised as a Benefits case in the HR portal. People Operations responds within 3 business days. The AI assistant and self-service tools provide policy information only; they do not make eligibility determinations. Final eligibility determinations are made by People Operations and plan administrators.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I'm a contractor. Am I eligible for health insurance through the Company?**
A: No. Independent contractors and agency workers are not eligible for Company benefits programs (Section 2.2); benefits, if any, come through your agency or service agreement.

**Q: When does my coverage start if I'm hired mid-month?**
A: The first day of the month after your hire date — coverage never begins mid-month (Section 2.3).

**Q: I missed the 30-day new-hire enrollment window. What now?**
A: You wait for open enrollment (November 1–15) unless you have a qualifying life event (Sections 4.2–4.3).

**Q: When does 401(k) matching start?**
A: You can contribute from your first paycheck; the 4% employer match begins after 90 days and vests immediately (Section 5).

**Q: Do my benefits continue during parental leave?**
A: Yes — benefits continue unchanged during any paid leave (Section 6.1).
