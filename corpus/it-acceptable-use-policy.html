LSJ, Inc. — IT Acceptable Use Policy

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets the rules for using LSJ systems: laptops, accounts, email, chat, video tools, code repositories, and cloud services. It complements the Data Security Policy (POL-007), which governs data protection, and the Code of Conduct (POL-005), which governs behavior. It applies to employees, contractors, and interns.

2. General Use and Ownership

Company systems exist for business use. Incidental personal use (brief personal email, news) is permitted if it does not interfere with work, consume significant resources, or violate any policy. All data created or stored on company systems is company property, except where law provides otherwise. Employees should have no expectation of privacy on company systems; the Company may monitor and audit system activity as permitted by law, with access to monitoring data restricted to authorized Security and Legal personnel.

3. Accounts and Authentication

  • Use only your own credentials; sharing accounts or passwords is prohibited (POL-007, Section 4).
  • Passwords must be stored in the company password manager, not in browsers, files, or notes.
  • MFA prompts you did not initiate must be denied and reported under POL-007, Section 9.

4. Email and Messaging

4.1 Professional standards

All communication standards of the Code of Conduct (POL-005, Sections 3 and 9) apply in email, chat, and video tools.

4.2 External email caution

Confidential and Restricted data (POL-007, Section 3) may not be sent to personal email accounts or unapproved external recipients. Auto-forwarding company email externally is technically blocked and prohibited.

4.3 Phishing

Report suspicious messages with the "Report Phish" button. Do not click links or open attachments from unverified senders. Reported messages are triaged by Security within 1 business day.

4.4 Retention

Email and chat are retained per the records schedule: general email 24 months, chat 12 months, unless a legal hold applies. Do not use personal channels (personal messaging apps) for substantive business decisions.

5. AI Tools

5.1 Approved tools

Only AI tools on the IT-approved list (published in the IT portal) may be used with company data. Approval requests follow POL-007, Section 8.

5.2 Data rules

Public/Internal data may be used with approved AI tools. Confidential data requires the tool to be on the approved list with an enterprise agreement. Restricted data may never be entered into AI tools, approved or otherwise.

5.3 Output responsibility

Employees remain responsible for work products produced with AI assistance, including accuracy, licensing, and security review of AI-generated code.

6. Software, Code, and Cloud

  • Install software only from the IT self-service catalog, or with an approved IT request (POL-007, Section 8).
  • Company source code lives only in company-managed repositories. Copying code to personal accounts or devices is prohibited.
  • Production access follows least privilege and the Restricted-data approval path (POL-010, Section 4).
  • Personal cloud storage accounts must not hold company files; use company-managed drives.

7. Prohibited Activities

Using company systems to: harass or discriminate (POL-005, Section 3), access or distribute illegal content, run unauthorized crypto-mining or personal businesses, circumvent security controls (POL-006, Section 6), scrape or exfiltrate company data, or knowingly introduce malware. This list is illustrative, not exhaustive.

8. Network Use

Bandwidth-heavy personal streaming on office networks is discouraged during business hours. Guest Wi-Fi is for visitors and personal devices; company devices use the corporate network or VPN (POL-007, Section 5.2). Personal devices may not join the corporate network outside MDM enrollment (POL-007, Section 7).

9. Enforcement

Suspected violations are investigated by Security with People Operations. Consequences follow the disciplinary process in POL-005, Section 10, and may include access revocation under POL-007, Section 10.
