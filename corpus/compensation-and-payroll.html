LSJ, Inc. — Compensation and Payroll Policy

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how LSJ pays employees: pay schedule, compensation structure, location-based bands, promotions, bonuses, and payroll corrections. Benefits are described in POL-004; expense reimbursement in POL-003.

2. Payroll Schedule

Employees are paid semi-monthly on the 15th and the last business day of each month. When a payday falls on a weekend or company holiday (POL-009), payment is made the preceding business day. Approved expense reimbursements ride the same runs (POL-003, Section 3.4). Pay statements are available in the HR portal.

3. Compensation Structure

3.1 Bands and levels

Every role maps to a job level (L1–L8 for individual contributors, M1–M4 for managers) with a published salary band. Bands are reviewed annually against market data effective April 1.

3.2 Location factors

Salary bands are location-based, keyed to the employee's registered work address. Three geographic tiers apply: Tier A (San Francisco, New York, London), Tier B (Austin HQ, Seoul, most U.S. metros), Tier C (other approved locations). Permanent relocation across tiers adjusts pay to the destination band (POL-002, Section 6). Temporary remote work under POL-002, Sections 4–5 does NOT change compensation.

3.3 Pay transparency

Employees may see their own band and level in the HR portal. Sharing your own compensation is protected activity; accessing others' compensation without authorization violates POL-013, Section 7.

4. Promotions and Adjustments

Promotion nominations occur in the two review cycles (POL-010, Section 2), effective January 1 and July 1. Out-of-cycle adjustments require department head and People Operations approval and are reserved for retention risk, market corrections, or scope changes.

5. Bonuses

  • Annual bonus: target percentage by level, funded by company performance, paid by March 15 for the prior year. Employees must be employed on the payment date to receive it.
  • Spot bonuses: $250–$2,500 for exceptional contributions, manager-initiated with department head approval.
  • Referral bonus: $2,000 for a successful referral who completes 90 days, paid on the referral's 90-day date.

6. Stipends and Premiums

  • On-call stipend: $150 per week of formal on-call rotation (POL-011, Section 8).
  • Non-exempt overtime and holiday premiums: see POL-011, Section 5 and POL-009, Section 5.
  • Home office stipends and internet allowance are expense programs under POL-003, Section 7, not payroll items.

7. Deductions and Withholding

Payroll withholds required taxes based on the registered work address — one reason temporary work-location changes require the notifications in POL-002 (Sections 4–5). Voluntary deductions include benefits premiums (POL-004), 401(k) contributions (POL-004, Section 5), and commuter benefits (POL-004, Section 8).

8. Payroll Errors and Questions

Suspected payroll errors should be filed as an HR case under category "Payroll" (POL-015, Section 3) within 60 days of the pay date. Confirmed underpayments are corrected with an off-cycle payment within 5 business days; overpayments are recovered on a repayment schedule agreed with the employee as permitted by law. General payroll questions are answered by People Operations within 3 business days.

9. Final Pay

On separation, final pay follows the timing required by local law and includes payout of accrued amounts only where required by law (POL-001, Section 9). Company property must be returned per POL-006, Section 7.
