LSJ, INC. — WORKING HOURS, OVERTIME, AND TIMEKEEPING POLICY
Doc ID: POL-011 | Category: Workplace | Version 1.8 | Last updated: 2026-02-02
Owner: People Operations
NOTE: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — fictional company, created for an academic project.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy defines standard working hours, core hours, overtime rules, and
timekeeping requirements for LSJ employees. It applies to all regular
employees. Contractor hours are governed by their service agreements.

2. STANDARD WORKING HOURS

2.1 The standard full-time schedule is 40 hours per week, Monday through
Friday. Departments may approve alternative schedules (for example 4x10) with
department head sign-off, provided core hours coverage (Section 3) is
maintained.

2.2 Part-time schedules of 20-29 hours per week are set in the employment
agreement and drive prorated PTO accrual (POL-001, Section 2.1) and benefits
eligibility (POL-004, Section 2.1).

3. CORE HOURS

Core collaboration hours are 10:00-16:00 local time for each employee's home
office, on all scheduled workdays. Meetings should be scheduled inside core
hours wherever possible. Employees temporarily working abroad must maintain at
least 4 hours of overlap with home-office core hours (POL-002, Section 5.5).

4. FLEXIBILITY

Outside core hours, employees may flex start and end times with manager
awareness. Sustained schedule changes of more than 2 hours from the standard
department schedule require manager approval in writing.

5. OVERTIME (NON-EXEMPT EMPLOYEES)

5.1 Non-exempt employees are paid 1.5x their regular rate for hours worked
over 40 in a workweek, and as otherwise required by applicable law.

5.2 All overtime must be approved by the direct manager IN ADVANCE. Working
unapproved overtime is a timekeeping violation, although the time worked will
still be paid as required by law.

5.3 Work on an observed company holiday is governed by the Holiday Calendar
(POL-009, Section 5): 1.5x pay plus a substitute day off, with department head
approval.

5.4 Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay. Sustained excessive
workload should be raised with the manager or through the escalation paths in
POL-010, Section 6.

6. TIMEKEEPING

6.1 Non-exempt employees record all time worked in the HR portal timesheet,
due by 12:00 on the Monday following each workweek. Managers approve
timesheets by end of day Tuesday.

6.2 Falsifying timesheets is fraud under the Code of Conduct (POL-005,
Section 7).

6.3 Exempt employees do not submit timesheets but must record full-day
absences (PTO, sick leave, floating holidays) in the HR portal so balances
stay accurate (POL-001).

7. BREAKS

Employees working 6 or more consecutive hours take at least a 30-minute
unpaid meal break. Local law prevails where it requires more. Nursing
employees have access to lactation rooms and paid break time as described in
the Workplace Accommodations and Safety Policy (POL-016, Section 5).

8. ON-CALL WORK

Roles with formal on-call rotations receive the on-call stipend defined in
the Compensation and Payroll Policy (POL-014, Section 6). On-call schedules
must give each participant at least 2 rotation-free weeks per month unless
the employee agrees otherwise in writing.
