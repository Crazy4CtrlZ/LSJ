LSJ, Inc. — Performance and Approval Processes (POL-010)

1. Purpose and Scope

This document describes performance review cycles, the introductory-period review, manager escalation paths, and the company-wide Approval Matrix referenced by other policies. It applies to all regular employees and their managers.

2. Performance Review Cycle

LSJ runs two review cycles per year: a mid-year check-in (June) and an annual review (December) that informs compensation planning. Reviews combine a self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback from 2–4 colleagues. Ratings use a four-point scale: Exceeds, Meets, Developing, Below. A "Below" rating triggers a performance improvement plan (Section 5).

3. Introductory Period Review

At 90 days, managers complete the introductory-period review covering the 30/60/90 plan outcomes (POL-006, Section 3.2). Successful completion ends the PTO usage restriction (POL-001, Section 2.3). Concerns at 90 days may extend the introductory period by up to 60 days with People Operations approval.

5. Performance Improvement Plans

PIPs run 30–60 days with written goals, weekly manager check-ins, and a People Operations partner assigned to the case. Outcomes are: successful completion, extension (once, up to 30 days), or separation.

6. Manager Escalation Paths

Employees who cannot resolve an issue with their direct manager may escalate to the department head or People Operations. Conduct-related concerns follow the reporting channels in the Code of Conduct (POL-005, Section 4) rather than the performance process. Questions the HR assistant cannot ground in policy text, and any request involving exceptions to the Approval Matrix, must be escalated to People Operations as an HR case.
