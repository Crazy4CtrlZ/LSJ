LSJ, Inc. — Holiday Calendar 2026 (POL-009)

1. Purpose and Scope

This document lists company-observed paid holidays for calendar year 2026 for all LSJ offices, plus the floating holiday program. Holidays interact with PTO as described in the PTO and Leave Policy (POL-001, Section 3.5): observed holidays inside an approved PTO period are not deducted from PTO balances.

3. International Office Substitutions

The London and Seoul offices observe eleven holidays per year, substituting local public holidays for U.S.-specific dates. Office-specific lists are published by local People Operations each December. Employees temporarily working abroad (POL-002, Section 5) continue to observe their home office's holiday calendar, not the destination country's.

4. Floating Holidays

All regular employees receive 2 floating holidays per calendar year, granted January 1 (prorated: 1 floating holiday for hires after July 1). Floating holidays:

  • may be used for cultural, religious, or personal observances of the employee's choice;
  • require the same notice as a 1–2 day PTO request (3 business days — POL-001, Section 3.1) and manager approval;
  • do not carry over and are not paid out.

5. Holiday Pay Rules

Holidays are paid at regular base pay for eligible employees. Non-exempt employees required to work on an observed holiday receive 1.5x pay plus a substitute day off within 30 days, with department head approval (POL-010, Section 4). Part-time employees are paid for holidays that fall on regularly scheduled workdays. Contractors and interns are not eligible for holiday pay.

6. Office Closure Weeks

The Company does not mandate a year-end shutdown in 2026. Teams wishing to schedule coordinated year-end coverage use normal PTO requests under POL-001; managers must ensure minimum coverage for customer-facing functions.
