# LSJ, Inc. — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines where LSJ employees may perform their work: hybrid arrangements, designated fully-remote roles, and temporary remote work from other locations, including internationally. It applies to all regular employees. Contractors follow the work-location terms of their service agreements.

All remote work is conditioned on compliance with the Data Security Policy (POL-007). Approval authority is defined in the Approval Matrix (POL-010, Section 4).

## 2. Hybrid Work (Default Arrangement)

### 2.1 Anchor days

Employees assigned to an office work a hybrid schedule with a minimum of 3 office days per week. Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are company-wide anchor days unless a department publishes an alternative schedule approved by its department head.

### 2.2 Home workspace

On non-office days, employees must work from their registered home address or another location within their employment jurisdiction that meets the security requirements of POL-007, Section 5 (secure network, screen privacy in public spaces, device encryption).

## 3. Designated Fully-Remote Roles

Some roles are designated fully remote at the time of hire or by later reclassification. Reclassification requires department head and People Operations approval. Fully-remote employees must maintain a registered work address in an approved employment jurisdiction; changing that address across state or country lines is a permanent relocation governed by Section 6, not by the temporary provisions of Sections 4 and 5.

## 4. Temporary Domestic Remote Work

Employees may work temporarily from another location within their country of employment (for example, another state) for up to 30 calendar days per calendar year, subject to:

1. Advance manager approval in the HR portal at least 5 business days before travel.
2. Notification to Workplace Operations, which reviews state/provincial tax registration implications for stays exceeding 14 consecutive days.
3. Continued compliance with POL-007 security requirements.

Days beyond the 30-day annual limit require the escalation process in Section 5.4.

## 5. Temporary International Remote Work

### 5.1 Annual limit

Employees may work remotely from outside their country of employment for a maximum of 20 business days in any rolling 12-month period.

### 5.2 Approval requirements

International remote work requires ALL of the following, requested at least 15 business days before departure:

1. Direct manager approval.
2. People Operations approval (checks employment-law and payroll-tax exposure).
3. A completed International Remote Work Request in the HR portal listing destination country, dates, and work address.

### 5.3 Security conditions

While abroad, employees must comply with POL-007, Section 6 (International Travel and Devices), including VPN use on all networks, device encryption verification before departure, and the clean-device program for roles handling Restricted data. Employees may not access Restricted-classification data from countries on the Restricted Country List maintained by Security (POL-007, Section 6.4).

### 5.4 Requests exceeding the limit

Requests exceeding 20 business days in a rolling 12-month period (for example, six weeks abroad) exceed the standard allowance and are generally not approved. Exceptions require Legal and Tax review initiated by People Operations, take up to 20 business days to evaluate, and are granted only for exceptional circumstances such as family emergencies or visa-mandated stays. Employees should file an HR case to begin an exception review rather than booking travel first.

### 5.5 Working hours

During temporary international remote work, employees must maintain at least 4 hours of overlap with their home team's core hours (10:00–16:00 home-office local time) unless the manager approves an alternative schedule in writing.

## 6. Permanent Relocation

Permanent moves to another state or country require a Relocation Request submitted at least 60 days in advance, and are subject to role eligibility, compensation-band adjustment, entity availability in the destination country, and approval by the department head and People Operations. Permanent relocation is out of scope for the temporary allowances in Sections 4 and 5.

## 7. Equipment and Expenses for Remote Work

Company equipment usage, home-office stipends, and internet reimbursement are governed by the Onboarding and Equipment Policy (POL-006, Sections 5–6) and the Expense and Reimbursement Policy (POL-003, Section 7). The Company does not ship equipment internationally for temporary remote work; employees travel with their assigned laptop under POL-007 travel rules.

## 8. Revocation

Managers may recall employees from any temporary remote arrangement with 5 business days' notice for business needs. Violations of this policy or of POL-007 may result in revocation of remote work privileges and disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct (POL-005).

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I work from another country for six weeks?**
A: Six weeks (about 30 business days) exceeds the 20-business-day rolling annual limit in Section 5.1, so the standard process cannot approve it. You would need the Section 5.4 exception path — Legal and Tax review via People Operations, generally granted only for exceptional circumstances. File an HR case before making any travel plans.

**Q: Does a business trip abroad count against my 20-day international remote work allowance?**
A: No — business travel is governed by the Travel Policy (POL-008, Section 8). But adding personal remote-work days to a business trip does count and needs Section 5.2 approvals in advance.

**Q: Can I extend a vacation abroad by working the following week from there?**
A: Yes, if the worked days fit within the Section 5.1 limit and you obtain Section 5.2 approvals at least 15 business days before departure. Vacation days themselves are PTO under POL-001.

**Q: Does temporary remote work change my pay?**
A: No. Only permanent relocation adjusts compensation bands (POL-014, Section 3.2).
