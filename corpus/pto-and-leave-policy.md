# LSJ, Inc. — PTO and Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes paid time off (PTO), sick leave, and extended leave programs for employees of LSJ, Inc. ("LSJ" or "the Company"). It applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees worldwide. Contractors, consultants, and interns are not eligible for PTO accrual or paid leave under this policy (see Section 8).

Company-observed holidays are governed separately by the Holiday Calendar (POL-009). Approval authority for time-off requests is defined in the Approval Matrix in the Performance and Approval Processes document (POL-010).

## 2. PTO Accrual

### 2.1 Accrual rates

Regular full-time employees accrue PTO monthly based on completed years of service:

| Years of service | Monthly accrual | Annual equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 1.25 days | 15 days |
| 3–5 years | 1.67 days | 20 days |
| 6+ years | 2.08 days | 25 days |

Part-time employees (20–29 scheduled hours per week) accrue PTO on a prorated basis relative to a 40-hour week.

### 2.2 Accrual timing

PTO accrues on the last calendar day of each month. Accrual begins on the first day of employment; however, see Section 2.3 for restrictions during the introductory period.

### 2.3 Introductory period

During the first 90 calendar days of employment, employees accrue PTO but may not use it, except for documented emergencies approved by both the manager and People Operations.

### 2.4 Carryover and forfeiture

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they are forfeited. PTO balances above the carryover cap are forfeited on December 31. The Company does not pay out forfeited PTO except where required by local law.

## 3. Requesting PTO

### 3.1 Notice requirements

PTO requests must be submitted in the HR portal with the following minimum notice:

| Request length | Minimum notice |
|---|---|
| 1–2 consecutive days | 3 business days |
| 3–9 consecutive days | 10 business days |
| 10 or more consecutive days | 20 business days |

Requests submitted with less notice than required may still be approved at the manager's discretion for unforeseen circumstances, but approval is not guaranteed.

### 3.2 Approval requirements

All PTO requests require direct manager approval. Requests of 10 or more consecutive days additionally require department head approval (see POL-010, Section 4, Approval Matrix). A request is not confirmed until it shows an "Approved" status in the HR portal.

### 3.3 Insufficient balance

Employees may not request more PTO than their current available balance plus the accrual expected before the requested dates. Negative PTO balances are not permitted. Employees with insufficient balance may request unpaid personal leave under Section 7.

### 3.4 Blackout periods

Departments may designate up to two blackout windows per year (for example, quarter-end close for Finance or launch weeks for Engineering) during which PTO approvals are limited. Blackout windows must be announced at least 60 days in advance.

### 3.5 PTO during company holidays

Company holidays that fall within an approved PTO period are not deducted from the PTO balance. See the Holiday Calendar (POL-009) for observed dates by office.

## 4. Sick Leave

Regular employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, granted in full on January 1 (prorated for mid-year hires). Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out. Sick leave covers personal illness, medical appointments, and care for an immediate family member.

A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days. Sick leave taken fraudulently is grounds for disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct (POL-005).

## 5. Parental Leave

Employees with at least 12 months of continuous service are eligible for:

- Primary caregiver leave: 12 weeks paid at 100% of base salary.
- Secondary caregiver leave: 4 weeks paid at 100% of base salary.

Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or foster placement, and may be split into at most two blocks. Employees should notify People Operations at least 30 days before the expected start of leave. Benefits continue during paid parental leave (see POL-004, Section 6).

## 6. Bereavement and Jury Duty

- Bereavement leave: up to 5 paid days for an immediate family member; up to 2 paid days for an extended family member.
- Jury duty: paid for the duration of service upon presentation of the summons; employees must notify their manager within 2 business days of receiving the summons.

## 7. Unpaid Personal Leave

Employees who have exhausted PTO may request unpaid personal leave of up to 30 calendar days per year. Unpaid leave requires manager and People Operations approval and is granted at the Company's discretion. Benefits continuation during unpaid leave is described in POL-004, Section 6.2.

## 8. Eligibility Exclusions

The following groups are not eligible for PTO, sick leave, or paid leave programs under this policy:

- Independent contractors and agency workers (per their service agreements).
- Interns, who instead receive unpaid time off by arrangement with their manager.

## 9. Separation

Upon separation, accrued unused PTO is paid out only where required by applicable law. Approved future-dated PTO is cancelled on the separation date.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I want to take 3 days off next week. Is that allowed?**
A: A 3-day request falls in the 3–9 consecutive day band, which requires 10 business days' notice (Section 3.1). A request for next week is inside the notice window, so it is not guaranteed; your manager may still approve it at their discretion for unforeseen circumstances (Section 3.1) if your balance covers it (Section 3.3).

**Q: Do holidays during my vacation count against my PTO?**
A: No. Observed holidays inside an approved PTO period are not deducted (Section 3.5).

**Q: Can I go negative on PTO and pay it back later?**
A: No. Negative balances are not permitted (Section 3.3). Consider unpaid personal leave (Section 7).

**Q: I'm in my first 90 days. Can I use the PTO I've accrued?**
A: Only for documented emergencies with manager and People Operations approval (Section 2.3).

**Q: How much PTO can I carry into next year?**
A: Up to 5 days, which must be used by March 31 (Section 2.4).
