HR CASE AND ESCALATION PROCEDURES — LSJ, INC.
Doc ID: POL-015 | Category: HR Operations | Version 1.5 | Last updated: 2026-06-20
Owner: People Operations

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This document describes how HR requests and concerns are filed, categorized, prioritized, and escalated at LSJ, including what self-service tools and the HR assistant may and may not do. It applies to all employees, contractors, and interns.

2. CHANNELS

1. *HR portal (primary)* — file an HR case, submit PTO and remote-work requests, view balances and pay statements.
2. *HR assistant* — answers policy questions with citations and can open cases on the employee's behalf (Section 6).
3. *People Operations partner* — direct contact for complex or sensitive matters.
4. *Ethics Line* — confidential and anonymous reporting channel for conduct concerns (POL-005, Section 4.1).

3. CASE CATEGORIES

  Category | Examples | First response SLA
  Time Off | PTO disputes, balance corrections | 3 business days
  Leave — Extended | Medical, caregiver, military, sabbatical (POL-012) | 2 business days
  Benefits | Eligibility, enrollment, claims (POL-004, Section 9) | 3 business days
  Payroll | Errors, deductions (POL-014, Section 8) | 3 business days
  Workplace | Remote work exceptions (POL-002, Section 5.4), accommodations intake | 3 business days
  Conduct — Sensitive | Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, safety (POL-005) | 2 business days, human-only
  Other | Anything else | 5 business days

SLAs are measured in the employee's home-office business days. Resolution targets vary by complexity and are communicated in the case.

4. PRIORITY AND ESCALATION

4.1 Priority levels

- *P1 — Urgent:* safety risks, imminent-harm concerns, payroll failures affecting a pay date. Acknowledged same business day.
- *P2 — High:* time-sensitive determinations (leave start dates, enrollment windows closing within 5 business days).
- *P3 — Standard:* everything else.

4.2 Escalation path

Cases unresolved past SLA escalate automatically: case owner → People Operations team lead → Director of People Operations. Employees may also escalate manually from the case view. Manager-related concerns follow POL-010, Section 6; conduct concerns always follow POL-005, Section 4.

5. SENSITIVE CASE HANDLING

Cases in the "Conduct — Sensitive" category are visible only to trained case managers and Legal. They are never handled by automated systems (POL-005, Section 4.2). Anonymous Ethics Line reports receive a case number allowing two-way anonymous follow-up.

6. WHAT AUTOMATED TOOLS MAY DO

The HR assistant and other self-service automation MAY:

1. Answer policy questions using official policy documents, with citations to document and section.
2. Look up the requesting employee's own profile, balances, and benefits status.
3. Draft messages (for example to a manager) for the employee to review and send.
4. Open a mock/draft HR case or ticket for the employee to confirm before submission.
5. Route the employee to the correct category, form, or human contact.

The HR assistant and automation MUST NOT:

1. Make final eligibility, approval, or disciplinary determinations (reserved to People Operations and the approvers in POL-010, Section 4).
2. Handle Conduct — Sensitive matters beyond acknowledging and routing to humans (POL-005, Section 4.2).
3. Modify balances, records, or elections directly.
4. Answer questions outside company policy scope; out-of-scope questions are declined with a referral to appropriate resources.
5. Access another employee's personal records for a requester without authorization.

7. DOCUMENTATION AND PRIVACY

Case contents are Confidential (POL-007, Section 3), retained per the records schedule, and accessible only to those with a need to know. Employees may request a copy of their own case history through a "Other" category case.
